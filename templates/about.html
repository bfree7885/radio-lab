About Radio Lab

Hands-on training

Radio Lab is a local workshop for learning amateur radio by working with frequencies, controls, diagrams, and scenarios. It is practice on this computer, not a textbook and not a question bank.

Each lab is meant to follow the same path: learn, see it, do it, explain it, connect it to the exam, then try a field task.

Where the curriculum starts

Technician is the first target. Technician Foundations V0.1 is the track on the dashboard. General comes later, after this track has real labs.

Hardware stays optional

The core application does not need a radio. Later versions may offer a live lab when hardware is actually available — an RTL-SDR, a scanner, an amateur radio, a DigiRig, an audio interface, or a GPS/GNSS receiver. If that hardware is absent, the lab continues in simulation.

Waypoint Deck integration is planned and separate. Deck is not part of this application, and Radio Lab does not require it.
